# PCB mechanical layout status

Proposed 95 × 125 mm board. The 12 MX-style key centres are recorded in `switch-placement.csv` at 19.05 mm pitch.

MCP23017 breakout envelope: 31 × 20 × 7.14 mm. Along-row pin pitch: 2.54 mm.

**Not yet established:** number of holes on each side, centre-to-centre distance between the two rows, first-hole offset, board thickness and total installed socket stack height. These prevent a verified module footprint.

The actual switch part number and its locating post/electrical pin offsets are also not yet established.

These files specify the mechanical plan and run meaningful placement checks. They are not an electrically verified PCB. KiCad ERC/DRC/3D-view checks have not been run. No manufacturing release.
